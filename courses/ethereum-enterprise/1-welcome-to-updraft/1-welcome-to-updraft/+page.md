## Core Web3 Concepts You Will Learn

Throughout this course, we will demystify the core pillars of the web3 ecosystem. You will gain a clear understanding of:

*   **Smart Contracts:** Self-executing digital agreements with the terms directly written into code.
*   **Node Operators:** The entities running the foundational software that keeps blockchain networks operational and secure.
*   **Decentralization:** The shift of control and decision-making away from central authorities to distributed networks.
*   **DeFi (Decentralized Finance):** Peer-to-peer financial services built entirely on blockchain technology.
*   **NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens):** Cryptographically unique digital assets verified on a public ledger.
*   **DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations):** Community-led entities governed by smart contracts and decentralized voting mechanisms.
